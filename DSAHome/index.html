How to use this home

Start with the Problem-Solving Playbook, then work a track at a time. For each topic: play the visualizer, predict the next step before pressing Next, re-type the template from memory, then climb the problem ladder until the hard problems feel inevitable. Mark a topic mastered only when you can explain its invariant out loud.
